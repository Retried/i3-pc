Working
  boot from the shortcut straight into the game, own icon and profile; rendering in every scene
  reached; sound (measured: no clipping, clicks or late buffers); keyboard and mouse incl. the
  motion gestures of the spray-can tutorial; key rebinding with matching icons; autosave,
  Continue, Load Game list; 60 FPS in light scenes.

Not verified or not right yet
  - Heavy scenes (cannery interior, crash site) run at the game's 30 FPS lock; 60 is not reached
    there. The render thread is near its limit. Light scenes (intro, billboard) reach 107-120 FPS
    with the 120 cap in Settings.bat; game speed at caps above 60 was not checked by eye.
  - Played up to just after Delsin gains his power (story cutscenes play, autosave works).
    Using the powers, combat, the city and later missions are still unplayed.
  - Touchpad swipe up (I key) works on a real prompt; down/left/right are untested.
  - After the power cutscene two wrecked cars are drawn flat bright orange. It may be the
    game's own highlight or a rendering fault; not yet compared with the PS4.
  - Loading a slot from the Load Game list: the log shows the save being read and the world
    loading; the resulting scene was not checked on screen.
  - PS4 Pro mode no longer crashes (a shader pattern the recompiler did not know) and outputs
    3840x2160, but the picture is wrong: green tint and a dither pattern from the Pro mode's
    checkerboard reconstruction. Settings.bat still does not offer it.
  - Xbox icon set exists (A/B/X/Y, LB/LT/RB/RT, LS/RS, MENU, VIEW); seen in the icon sheet,
    not yet seen in game with a real Xbox pad.
  - A rare bad draw at scene load used to crash the graphics device; it is now dropped
    (about 40 boots since without a crash), root cause unknown.
  - Sound has been measured, not listened to.

Sound check, 2026-10-10 02:35 (optimized build, 30 s of walking, jumping and camera movement
outdoors, recorded from the program's audio output): 30.02 s of audio against 30.00 s of
video; no silent gaps of 1 ms or more; no click (sample jump far above the local level); no
clipped sample (peak 0.19 of full scale); no repeated mixer block; both channels alike
(RMS 0.023 / 0.024). This is a measurement, not a listening test: wrong or missing sounds
(a line of speech not playing, a wrong effect) cannot be found this way.

Red tint while standing in burning brush: checked at full resolution, all 256 colour levels in
use and as smooth as a normal frame, so it is the game's own effect, not a rendering fault.

Play progress, 2026-10-10 04:55 (keyboard only, optimized build): through the cannery gate
(Smoke Dash), into the building through the wall vents and the skylight, the cutscene inside,
and the first combat tutorial (chain melee with F breaks both timber barriers). Screenshots
19 to 24. One device loss (GPU hang, then lost) happened in that scene before the indirect
draw hardening; three runs of the same scene on the hardened build survived. The cause is
not proven, so a rare crash there cannot be ruled out.

Stability, 2026-10-10 16:33 (replaces the earlier notes on this): every crash seen since the
night build of 01:11 had one cause, a speed-up of mine. Results that GPU compute steps produce
are copied back into game memory; I had made that copy not wait for the GPU, so it landed a
frame late, on memory the game had already reused. That gave garbage particle draw counts
(device losses in burning areas) and, with sustained shooting in the Hank fight, an access
violation after about two and a half minutes. Proof, three minutes of continuous shooting:
with the wait 0 garbage draws; without it 31 on the test build, 548 and the crash on the
optimized build. The copy waits again (about 2 FPS in heavy scenes). On the optimized build
with the fix: three minutes of shooting and five minutes in the burning room, 0 garbage draws,
no fault, 60 FPS. That build is the shortcut's program since 16:33. The guards added while
chasing this (argument checks on indirect draws and dispatches) stay in.
